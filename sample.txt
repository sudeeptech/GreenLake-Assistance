HPE GreenLake Knowledge Base

Question: How to reset password in HPE GreenLake?
Answer:
Follow these steps to reset your password:
1. Go to the GreenLake login page: https://greenlake.cloud/login
2. Click "Forgot Password".
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Check your email and click the reset link.
5. Create a new password with:
   - Minimum 12 characters
   - One uppercase letter
   - One lowercase letter
   - One number
   - One special character
6. Click Submit and login with new password.

Question: How to add service subscription to workspace?
Answer:
1. Login to GreenLake Cloud.
2. Click Global Navigation Menu.
3. Go to Services → Manage Service Subscription.
4. Click Add Service Subscription.
5. Enter subscription key received via email.
6. Assign tags if needed.
7. Review details and click Finish.

Prerequisites:
- Workspace Administrator role required.
- Service must be set up.
- Subscription key required.

--------------------------------------------------

Question: How to convert workspace to MSP mode?
Answer:
1. Login as workspace administrator.
2. Navigate to Workspace Menu → Manage Workspace.
3. Click Manage Workspace Type.
4. Complete required steps:
   - Check eligibility
   - Remove assignments and subscriptions
   - Remove unsupported services
   - Convert workspace
5. Click "Convert to MSP mode" button.
6. Confirm conversion.

--------------------------------------------------

Question: How to invite users to workspace?
Answer:
1. Go to Manage Workspace.
2. Open Workspace Identity & Access.
3. Click Invite Users.
4. Send sign-up link via email.

--------------------------------------------------

Question: How to delete a user?
Answer:
1. Go to Manage Workspace.
2. Open Workspace Identity & Access → Users.
3. Select the user.
4. Click Delete and confirm.

--------------------------------------------------

Question: How to assign devices to service?
Answer:
1. Go to Devices → Devices Inventory.
2. Select devices.
3. Click Assign to Service.
4. Select workspace and service instance.
5. Finish assignment.

--------------------------------------------------

Question: How to remove device subscription?
Answer:
1. Go to Devices → Devices Inventory.
2. Open Assigned & Subscribed.
3. Select device.
4. Click Remove Assignment.

--------------------------------------------------

Question: How to disable MSP mode?
Answer:
1. Login as administrator.
2. Go to Manage Workspace → Manage Workspace Type.
3. Review and delete tenant workspaces.
4. Click Convert to Standard Enterprise Mode.
5. Confirm conversion.
