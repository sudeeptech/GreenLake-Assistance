Create an HPE account
To create an HPE account:

Use the appropriate method below:

HPE GreenLake Platform:

On the HPE GreenLake Sign In page, click Create account.

Non HPE GreenLake Platform:

On the HPE Sign In page, click Sign up.

Alternatively, go directly to the Create an HPE Account page: https://auth.hpe.com/hpe/cf/registration.

Fill in the required fields, and click Create Account.
You will receive a verification email. Click the Activate HPE Account link in the email. If you do not receive the email, check Troubleshooting.
If you need additional help, contact support.


If you have forgotten your password, use the appropriate reset method below:

If you are an HPE GreenLake Platform customer, or use any service that you access by first logging on to HPE GreenLake Platform, see Forgotten password - HPE GreenLake Platform.

If you are not using the HPE GreenLake Platform, see Forgotten password - Non HPE GreenLake Platform.

Forgotten password - HPE GreenLake Platform
Use this procedure if using HPE GreenLake Platform:

Click the Need help signing in? > Forgot password? link, or go directly to: https://auth.hpe.com/signin/forgot-password.

Passwords expire every 365 days. To change your password:

Navigate to your profile: https://auth.hpe.com/profile/UserProfile.

If you've forgotten your password, see I've forgotten my password.

In the Password section, click Edit.

Type your current and new password.

Click Reset Password, or click Cancel to discard your changes.

If you need additional help, contact support.


Use the password reset option:

On the Sign In page, click the Unlock Account? link, or go directly to: https://auth.hpe.com/signin/unlock.

Type your email address into the field.

Click Send Email. You will receive an email with instructions to unlock your account. If you do not receive the email, check Troubleshooting.

If you need additional help, contact support.
How do I set up multifactor authentication?
Multifactor Authentication (MFA) increases your account security when signing in to HPE.

To enable MFA, navigate to your profile: https://auth.hpe.com/profile/UserProfile.

In the Multifactor Authentication section, click the toggle button to turn on Multifactor Authentication.

Set up one of the following authentication methods:

Okta Verify

Use a push notification sent to the mobile app on your mobile device.

Security Key or Biometric Authenticator

Use a security key (USB or Bluetooth) or a biometric authenticator (Windows Hello, Touch ID).

Google Authenticator

Enter single-use code from the mobile app on your mobile device.


The administrator of your company's email domain can set up Single Sign-On (SSO) with HPE GreenLake. If you are signing in with your company's credentials, use the Sign in with SSO button.

Contact your organization’s HPE business representative or your email administrator for help with setting up an account.
